I am a PhD student in computational astrophysics and cosmology at Seoul National University, where I work with [Ji-hoon Kim](https://physics.snu.ac.kr/cosmo/index/index.html). My research interests are cosmological simulations, dwarf galaxies, ultra-faint dwarfs, machine learning, disk formation, direct N-body methods, and star cluster formation.
